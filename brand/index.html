YZE Typeface

YZE is a geometric typeface designed for personal identity, editorial layouts and digital interfaces.

Basic forms, perfect circles and controlled curves retain clarity across display and interface scales.

01 / Principles

Built from basic forms, refined for maximum legibility.

Geometric

Letters are informed by circles, straight lines and controlled curves.

Legible

Distinct letterforms reduce ambiguity across interfaces and body text.

Technical

Digital in character without becoming cryptic or difficult to read.

02 / Hierarchy

Grey supports. White emphasises. Blue identifies interaction.
