YAY —
Yacht Just Chillin'

Live your dream of sleeping out on the ocean in a luxury cabin cruiser. Yacht "Just Chillin'" features two staterooms each with comfy queen beds, two full bathrooms, covered patio, air conditioning, hot water, satellite WiFi, full dining indoor and outdoor, and more!

The yacht is anchored out on the ocean — watch waterfront sunsets and sunrises, and see dolphins, turtles and manatees right from the boat! Water Taxi Shuttles to the island of Key West are available sunrise to sunset. Enjoy this truly unique experience!
