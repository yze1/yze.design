Sightseeing

Key West Destinations

From remote sandbars to Civil War forts — every charter is a new adventure.

Sand Bars

Accessible only by boat — white sand beaches, ankle-deep water, massive party! Visit Snipe Point, Jewfish Basin, Mud Key & more!

Historic Seaport

The most famous part of Key West — classic restaurants like Schooner Wharf and Conch Republic, and the biggest yachts at Key West Bight Marina.

Fort Zachary

A famous pre-Civil War National Historic Monument with gorgeous beach, deli, picnic tables and the best views in Key West. A can't-miss National Park.

Sunset Key

The southernmost island in the United States — accessible only by ferry. Be sure to book the resort at Latitudes in advance!

Boca Grande

The last stop before the open channel to Dry Tortugas National Park. Empty beaches, turquoise water, even dolphins. Last spot with cell service before Fort Jefferson!

Dry Tortugas

The gem of the Florida Keys and Lower Caribbean — Fort Jefferson is a Civil War-era fortress. Lighthouse Key and Windjammer Wrecks are also must-sees.

Sand Key Lighthouse

Only 7 miles due south of Key West — snorkel or scuba dive the ruins of an old island dock and lighthouse with thousands of fish and wildlife.

Mangrove Manatees

The Sea Cow — nature's gentle giants. Cruise through covered mangrove canopies to find herds of these friendly ocean cattle.

Sunset @ Mallory Square

The most famous event in Key West — every evening at sunset at the southernmost place on earth with street performers, vendors and a free festival.

The Hemingway House

The home of Ernest Hemingway, Key West's own literary celebrity. Guided tours and endless six-toed cats!

Butterfly Museum

A magical indoor terrarium full of thousands of butterflies, flamingos, birds, and a gorgeous tropical botanical garden — a Key West local secret!

Southernmost Point

The most southern point in the continental USA. Lines form for pictures so go early! Visit the nearby Southernmost Mansion for rich Key West history.

Split Rock (Destroyer Key)

This key was split in two when a Navy Destroyer tore through it — creating a spectacular protected reef and beach for snorkeling and BBQs.

Duval Street

The most famous commercial strip in Florida — one mile from Gulf to Atlantic, lined with live music, outdoor restaurants, street vendors and all the vibrant colors of Key West!
