Off-Grid Identity 2024

The visual identity & product design for an AI powered design generator.

A close up of the packaging net. The intention behind the identity was to replicate the futuristic, idealistic aesthetic of the early 2000s: As new technologies were developed, manufacturers overpromised and underdelivered. This approach aligns with my personal view on AI, just as current technology benefits efficiency, it does not operate independently.

I believe humans will continue to be required to oversee AI outputs and we will never achieve the fully automated utopian/ dystopian future that is being promised. Read more about my thoughts on the future of AI here or an in depth explanation of the inner workings of AI here.

A poster designed for the product, focusing on the idealistic, futuristic aesthetic of the early 2000s and a short video explaining design principles: The foundation behind a successful, fully-automated design system.
